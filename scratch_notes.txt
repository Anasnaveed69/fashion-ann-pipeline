# Pipeline Scratch Notes
Temporary notes on dataset exploration:
- 10 classes in Fashion-MNIST: T-shirt/top, Trouser, Pullover, Dress, Coat, Sandal, Shirt, Sneaker, Bag, Ankle boot.
- 60,000 train images, 10,000 test images.
- Pixel intensity ranges from 0 to 255 grayscale.
- Needs normalization to [0, 1] before feeding into Dense layers.
